# Daily Language Pack (2026-11-09)

## Spanish
Words:
- libro — book
- ventana — window
- mesa — table
Phrases:
- leer un libro — to read a book
- cerca de la ventana — near the window
- sobre la mesa — on the table
Sentences:
- Leo un libro interesante junto a la ventana clara. — I read an interesting book next to the clear window.
- Mi libro favorito está sobre la mesa de madera. — My favorite book is on the wooden table.
- Coloco el libro nuevo cerca de la ventana luminosa. — I place the new book near the bright window.
