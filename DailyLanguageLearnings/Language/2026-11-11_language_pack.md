# Daily Language Pack (2026-11-11)

## Spanish
Words:
- carne — meat
- fruta — fruit
- verdura — vegetable
Phrases:
- comprar carne fresca — buy fresh meat
- comer frutas saludables — eat healthy fruits
- preparar verduras asadas — prepare roasted vegetables
Sentences:
- Compro carne y verdura en el mercado cada martes. — I buy meat and vegetables at the market every Tuesday.
- Mi familia come fruta fresca después de la comida. — My family eats fresh fruit after the meal.
- La verdura es saludable y la carne es deliciosa juntas. — The vegetable is healthy and the meat is delicious together.
