# Daily Language Pack (2026-08-07)

## Spanish
Words:
- ventana — window
- agua — water
- pan — bread
Phrases:
- beber agua fría — drink cold water
- abrir la ventana — open the window
- comprar pan fresco — buy fresh bread
Sentences:
- Abro la ventana por la mañana y bebo agua fresca. — I open the window in the morning and drink fresh water.
- Mi hermano come pan tostado con agua tibia cada día. — My brother eats toasted bread with warm water every day.
- La ventana grande permite ver el agua del río desde casa. — The large window allows you to see the river water from home.
