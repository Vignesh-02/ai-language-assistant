# Daily Language Pack (2026-11-05)

## Spanish
Words:
- puerta — door
- pan — bread
- noche — night
Phrases:
- abrir la puerta — open the door
- comer pan fresco — eat fresh bread
- buenas noches amigos — good night friends
Sentences:
- La puerta está cerrada durante la noche oscura. — The door is closed during the dark night.
- Abro la puerta y huelo el pan recién horneado. — I open the door and smell the freshly baked bread.
- Por la noche como pan tostado con mantequilla. — At night I eat toasted bread with butter.
