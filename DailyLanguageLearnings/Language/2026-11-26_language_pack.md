# Daily Language Pack (2026-11-26)

## Spanish
Words:
- carne — meat
- pollo — chicken
- verdura — vegetable
Phrases:
- carne asada deliciosa — delicious roasted meat
- pollo a la parrilla — grilled chicken
- verdura fresca y cruda — fresh raw vegetable
Sentences:
- La carne de pollo es nutritiva y saludable para comer. — Chicken meat is nutritious and healthy to eat.
- Compré pollo fresco y verdura en el mercado esta tarde. — I bought fresh chicken and vegetable at the market this afternoon.
- La carne y la verdura son ingredientes principales de mi cena. — The meat and vegetable are main ingredients of my dinner.
