# Daily Language Pack (2026-08-01)

## Spanish
Words:
- libro — book
- mesa — table
- silla — chair
Phrases:
- leer un libro — to read a book
- sentarse en la silla — to sit in the chair
- poner en la mesa — to put on the table
Sentences:
- Leo un libro interesante en la silla cómoda. — I read an interesting book in the comfortable chair.
- Mi libro favorito está sobre la mesa de madera. — My favorite book is on the wooden table.
- Ella se sienta en la silla y lee su libro nuevo. — She sits in the chair and reads her new book.
