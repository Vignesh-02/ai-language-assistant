# Daily Language Pack (2026-11-19)

## Spanish
Words:
- carne — meat
- ensalada — salad
- pollo — chicken
Phrases:
- carne asada deliciosa — delicious grilled meat
- ensalada fresca y verde — fresh and green salad
- pollo a la plancha — grilled chicken
Sentences:
- Compré carne fresca en el mercado para la comida de hoy. — I bought fresh meat at the market for today's meal.
- La ensalada verde acompaña bien con pollo asado. — The green salad pairs well with roasted chicken.
- Mi familia come pollo y ensalada tres veces por semana. — My family eats chicken and salad three times per week.
