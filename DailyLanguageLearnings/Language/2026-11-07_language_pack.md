# Daily Language Pack (2026-11-07)

## Spanish
Words:
- leche — milk
- queso — cheese
- sal — salt
Phrases:
- taza de leche caliente — cup of hot milk
- queso fresco del mercado — fresh cheese from the market
- agregar sal al gusto — add salt to taste
Sentences:
- La leche fresca y el queso son ingredientes básicos en mi cocina. — Fresh milk and cheese are basic ingredients in my kitchen.
- Bebo una taza de leche tibia antes de dormir cada noche. — I drink a cup of warm milk before sleeping each night.
- El queso salado es perfecto con pan tostado por la mañana. — Salty cheese is perfect with toasted bread in the morning.
