# Daily Language Pack (2026-08-16)

## Spanish
Words:
- mesa — table
- silla — chair
- libro — book
Phrases:
- sentarse en la silla — sit in the chair
- leer un libro — read a book
- poner en la mesa — put on the table
Sentences:
- Me siento en la silla para leer mi libro favorito. — I sit in the chair to read my favorite book.
- Pongo el libro sobre la mesa de madera cada noche. — I put the book on the wooden table every night.
- La silla está cerca de la mesa en la sala. — The chair is near the table in the living room.
