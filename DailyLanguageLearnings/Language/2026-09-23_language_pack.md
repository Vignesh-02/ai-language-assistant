# Daily Language Pack (2026-09-23)

## Spanish
Words:
- ventana — window
- silla — chair
- agua — water
Phrases:
- abrir la ventana — open the window
- sentarse en la silla — sit in the chair
- beber agua fresca — drink fresh water
Sentences:
- Abro la ventana para que entre aire fresco en la silla. — I open the window so fresh air enters near the chair.
- Mi hermano se sienta en la silla y bebe agua con hielo. — My brother sits in the chair and drinks water with ice.
- La ventana da luz a la silla donde descanso cada tarde. — The window provides light to the chair where I rest each afternoon.
