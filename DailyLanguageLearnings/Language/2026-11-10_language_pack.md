# Daily Language Pack (2026-11-10)

## Spanish
Words:
- silla — chair
- agua — water
- mano — hand
Phrases:
- sentarse en la silla — sit in the chair
- beber agua fría — drink cold water
- lavarse las manos — wash one's hands
Sentences:
- Mi mano sostiene la silla mientras bebo agua. — My hand holds the chair while I drink water.
- Lavo mis manos con agua limpia antes de comer. — I wash my hands with clean water before eating.
- La silla de madera tiene un apoyo firme para mis manos. — The wooden chair has firm support for my hands.
