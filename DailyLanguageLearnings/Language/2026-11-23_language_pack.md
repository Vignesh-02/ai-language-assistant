# Daily Language Pack (2026-11-23)

## Spanish
Words:
- cebolla — onion
- patata — potato
- aceite — oil
Phrases:
- sopa de verduras — vegetable soup
- cocinar en casa — cook at home
- mercado fresco — fresh market
Sentences:
- La cebolla y la patata son ingredientes básicos para la sopa. — Onion and potato are basic ingredients for soup.
- Cocino con aceite fresco en mi cocina cada día. — I cook with fresh oil in my kitchen every day.
- Compré cebolla, patata y aceite en el mercado fresco. — I bought onion, potato, and oil at the fresh market.
