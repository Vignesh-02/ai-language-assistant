# Daily Language Pack (2026-11-02)

## Spanish
Words:
- libro — book
- mesa — table
- silla — chair
Phrases:
- leer un libro — to read a book
- sentarse en la silla — to sit in the chair
- sobre la mesa — on the table
Sentences:
- El libro está sobre la mesa en la sala. — The book is on the table in the living room.
- Me siento en la silla para leer mi libro favorito. — I sit in the chair to read my favorite book.
- Coloco el libro sobre la mesa antes de sentarme. — I place the book on the table before sitting down.
