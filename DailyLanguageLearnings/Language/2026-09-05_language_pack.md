# Daily Language Pack (2026-09-05)

## Spanish
Words:
- sopa — soup
- cuchara — spoon
- taza — cup
Phrases:
- tomar una taza de café — drink a cup of coffee
- sopa caliente por la tarde — hot soup in the afternoon
- revolver la sopa con cuchara — stir the soup with a spoon
Sentences:
- La sopa está deliciosa y la taza es grande. — The soup is delicious and the cup is large.
- Revuelvo la sopa con la cuchara en el plato hondo. — I stir the soup with the spoon in the deep plate.
- Mi taza de café es perfecta para beber por la mañana. — My coffee cup is perfect for drinking in the morning.
